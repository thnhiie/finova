Your Financial Goals. Your Plan. Your Future.

FINOVA is an intelligent personal finance planning platform that transforms complex financial ambitions into clear, actionable roadmaps.

By combining AI-assisted goal parsing with a deterministic financial calculation engine, FINOVA helps you evaluate financial health, model multi-scenario cash flows, and stress-test your strategy against changes in income, savings rates, and assumptions.

What can FINOVA do for you?

Your finances, made easier to understand.

From understanding your current financial health to comparing possible futures, FINOVA brings your financial picture into one clear workspace.

⌁

01. Intelligent Financial Analysis

Organize income, expenses, cash flow, assets, and liabilities. Identify missing details and review your monthly financial position before planning.

◫

02. Multi-Scenario Planning

Compare five illustrative trajectories: Severely Conservative, Conservative, Baseline, Optimistic, and Highly Optimistic, using explicit mathematical assumptions.

↗

Adjust income, expenses, and savings assumptions to understand their effect on cash flow, savings capacity, and financial resilience.

✧

04. AI-Powered Financial Insights

Understand trade-offs, risk factors, constraints, and alternatives in plain language. Recommendations must be grounded in available data and assumptions.

⚖ Disclaimer & Safety Notice
FINOVA is a financial planning support platform designed strictly for educational and informational purposes. All calculations, projections, and scenario outcomes depend on user-provided information and stated assumptions. Results are estimates, not guarantees, certified financial audits, or personalized investment advice. FINOVA does not guarantee investment yields or recommend purchasing or selling specific financial products, securities, or assets.

Detailed interpretation

The Baseline scenario uses the figures entered in your financial profile. Conservative scenarios reduce income and increase expenses to test resilience. Optimistic scenarios assume higher income and, in the highly optimistic case, slightly lower expenses. Compare the monthly cash-flow differences with your debt obligations, liquidity needs, risk tolerance, and personal priorities before choosing a plan.

Goal timelines and exact goal shortfalls require a defined target amount and goal timeframe. Enter these on the Savings & Investment page to calculate goal-specific estimates.
